Goal: Create a "ROT13 Encoder/Decoder" feature.
Criteria:
1. Two connected textareas (like Base64Encoder): one for plain text, one for ROT13 encoded text.
2. Changes to plain text should instantly encode and populate the ROT13 textarea.
3. Changes to the ROT13 textarea should instantly decode (which is just ROT13 again) and populate the plain text textarea.
4. Integrate the new component (`Rot13Encoder`) into the application through `src/lib/tools.tsx` in the "Text & Encoding" category.
5. Provide tests to verify the component renders and correctly encodes/decodes (both lower and upper case, ignores numbers/punctuation).
