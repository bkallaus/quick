Phase 2: Think Before Coding
Assumptions: User wants a simple, single-page React component to calculate monthly mortgage payments based on principal, interest rate, and term.
Interpretations/Variance:
1. Standard Approach: A form with inputs for Principal, Rate, and Term, and a card displaying the Monthly Payment, Total Interest, and Total Payment.
2. Minimalist Approach: Just two inputs (Loan amount, monthly payment) to calculate how long it takes to pay off.
3. Lateral/Creative Approach: A visual amortization schedule with charts.

Chosen Approach: Standard Approach. It provides the most immediate value for a "quick calculations" app without over-complicating the UI or requiring charting libraries (Simplicity First).
