*   **Assumptions Explicitly Stated:** No explicit feature was requested by the user, only the instruction to find and build a "net-new functionality" and avoid duplication.
*   **Handling Multiple Interpretations:** I must choose a new calculation tool that fits the "Quick Calculations" theme. Options: Temperature Converter, Salary to Hourly, Aspect Ratio Calculator. I will choose Temperature Converter as it is universally useful and easy to verify.
*   **Forced Variance (Lateral Thinking) - 3 Approaches for Temperature Converter:**
    1.  *Standard:* 3 number inputs (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin). Typing in one instantly updates the other two.
    2.  *Minimalist:* 1 input field and two select dropdowns (convert From -> To).
    3.  *Lateral/Creative:* A visual, draggable thermometer.
    *Decision:* Approach 1 (Standard) is the simplest, provides the best UX (no clicking dropdowns), and aligns perfectly with "Simplicity First".
